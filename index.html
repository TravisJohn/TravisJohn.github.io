About

I'm a Senior BI Developer at WooliesX (Woolworths Group), where I work on product, campaign and executive analytics for the Everyday Rewards app on Google Cloud.

My work covers medallion data models on BigQuery, Airflow data-quality pipelines, A/B testing with significance testing, and reporting that goes to the CEO.

I came to data through finance. I'm a CPA with a Bachelor of Accounting, and before WooliesX I automated board and month-end reporting at Omnicom Media Group, humm group, Crop Smart and Qantas. I finished a Master of Data Science at UNSW in 2025.

I'm now moving into AI engineering. I build my own LLM applications with tool calling, RAG and agentic workflows, and I ship them through Git-based CI/CD built with AI coding agents.
