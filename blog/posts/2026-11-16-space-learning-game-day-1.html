Build in Public, Day 1: What If Learning Felt Like Playing?

This is Day 1 of a new Build in Public project. It isn’t part of OpenProbe. It’s a game I want to build to help me learn.

Inspiration

Yesterday I caught myself wondering whether there’s a fun way to learn something new.

I pictured myself travelling through space with nothing to do except keep the ship running. To clear each milestone, I’d have to finish a learning activity, maybe a coding task or an AWS task.

The problem

This build is well outside my comfort zone. I’m risking my Claude tokens on it, but I want to make something that actually helps me learn.

The solution

I’m using Fable 5.2 on Extra effort, and I’m writing very detailed prompts to get the look I’m after.

The story of the game

Your brain, or your consciousness, has been uploaded into a drone. You’re travelling to a nearby star, and along the way you have to keep the ship running by completing “learning” activities.

Fix the ship, learn the skill, reach the star.

Get involved

I’m planning to open source this. If you’re interested, send me a DM on LinkedIn.

And if you’re into NBA analytics, come say hi on my YouTube channel, NoFlightZone.
