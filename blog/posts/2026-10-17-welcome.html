Welcome: notes on data, BI and building with AI

Welcome. I am Travis John Villanueva, a Sydney-based Senior BI Developer at WooliesX and a Master of Data Science graduate from UNSW. I also have a background in accounting as a CPA.

I am starting this blog as a place to write short, practical notes as I keep growing my work from data and BI into AI engineering. Expect posts about lessons from building data pipelines and dashboards, what works (and what does not) when building LLM apps with tool calling and RAG, and notes from the AI apps I build, such as Hey Hugh, Whitepaper and Hardin.

This is the starter post. More posts are coming — please check back, or connect with me on LinkedIn.
